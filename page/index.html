About Hyderabad

Hyderabad is the capital city of Telangana and one of India's fastest-growing metropolitan cities. It is famous for Charminar, Golconda Fort, Ramoji Film City, Hussain Sagar Lake, delicious Biryani, rich culture, and world-class IT companies.
